          About us

          We are a Tamil Nadu taxi service built around one simple idea: travelling between towns should be easy to plan and fair to pay for. Whether you are catching a 5 a.m. flight from Chennai, visiting family in Tirunelveli or taking parents on a temple circuit through Thanjavur and Kumbakonam, we want you to know the likely cost before you book and to trust the car that turns up.

          Our fleet runs from compact hatchbacks for solo trips to 12-seater Tempo Travellers for group tours. Every booking is confirmed personally by phone, so you can talk through pickup points, stops on the way and luggage before the journey starts.

          We cover all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu and nearby Puducherry, with drivers who know the highways, the ghat roads up to Ooty and Kodaikanal, and the busy city streets alike.

          Privacy Policy

          This website does not store your details on any server. When you book, the information you enter is placed into an email or WhatsApp message on your own device, and it reaches us only when you choose to send it.

          We use your name, phone number, email and trip details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange your taxi and contact you about that trip. We do not sell or share your details with third parties, except with the driver assigned to your trip.

          The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. To ask us to delete any message you have sent, contact us using the details on this page.

          Terms & Conditions

          Sending a booking enquiry does not confirm a booking. A booking is confirmed only after we call or message you to confirm the vehicle, driver and fare.

          Fares shown on this website are approximate estimates based on straight-line distance between locations with a road-distance allowance. The final fare is based on actual distance travelled and may include toll charges, parking charges, interstate permits, waiting time and hill-station charges where applicable.

          Please share accurate pickup details and contact information. Cancellations and changes should be communicated as early as possible by phone or WhatsApp.
